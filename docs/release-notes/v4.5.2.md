**Fleet stops polling when the sidebar is collapsed on This Mac.** 4.5.1 polled
whenever the window was on screen, but with the Devices sidebar collapsed and This
Mac selected, no fleet data is shown anywhere — and the machines were still polled
every 3 s. Now they are polled only while something shows them: the sidebar (each
row is a machine's live summary), the Fleet overview, or a machine's page. Collapse
the sidebar on This Mac and every agent connection closes; open it, or switch to
Fleet, and polling resumes at once, with a break in the charts for the time in
between. ([#71](https://github.com/kennss/SiliconScope/issues/71))
